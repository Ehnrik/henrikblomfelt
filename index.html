About

I’m a Zürich-based musician and producer. I release solo work as Blomfelt and Evigt, and run my own imprint, Quiet Valley. My debut solo release as Blomfelt, “i’m interested in apathy”, came out via Oath in February 2024.

I’m also one half of Blomfelt & Narby, whose releases have appeared on Atomnation and Mesh, including the album Peter, Barbara, Beth & Friends. I previously produced and hosted Supportive Friends on Bristol station Noods.

Through my studio, Studio Felvar, I work in sound design, audio editing, mixing and mastering. My work on the Overkill podcast for Swiss magazine Der Beobachter was nominated for a series award at the Suisse Podcast Awards 2026.
